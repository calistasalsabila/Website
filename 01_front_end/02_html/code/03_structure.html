Omniscient Reader Viewpoint

Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint begins with the silent figure of Kim Dokja, the only reader who has walked through countless scenarios and endured endless despair. He lies in slumber, untouched by the chaos of the world. The others fight, bleed, and struggle, yet Dokja remains still— as if time itself has abandoned him.

The readers know his story better than anyone else.
They remember the sacrifices, the countless bargains, and the unshakable loneliness of a man who read until the very last line. They whisper his name, hoping that a single call might stir him awake.

But reality is cruel. Kim Dokja does not wake. His breath is faint, his presence almost fading into nothingness. The reader’s wishing, that he would open his eyes again. Yet the cruel narrative declares: he may never wake again.

Still, hope is stubborn. As long as Omniscient Reader’s Viewpoint is remembered, as long as the name Kim Dokja is spoken, there remains a fragile belief that one day, against all despair, he will rise once more.
